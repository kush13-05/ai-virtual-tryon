# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 2026_

This document explains what data the **NextGen AI Virtual Try-On Studio** app collects, how it's used, and how long it's kept.

## What data we collect

When you use this app, you upload two images:
1. A photo of yourself (used to generate the try-on result)
2. A photo of a garment you want to try on

We do **not** collect your name, email, location, or any other personal information. No account or sign-up is required to use the app.

## How your photos are used

Your uploaded photos are used **only** to generate the virtual try-on result. They are:

- Sent to a third-party AI model (hosted on Hugging Face) to generate the try-on image
- Temporarily saved on the server only for the duration of processing your request

## How long we keep your photos

**Your photos are not stored.** They are deleted from the server automatically immediately after each request completes — whether the request succeeds or fails. No copy is retained, logged, or used for any purpose beyond generating your result.

## Third-party services

This app sends your uploaded images to a third-party AI model hosted on **Hugging Face** (huggingface.co) in order to generate the try-on result. Please refer to [Hugging Face's own privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy) for how they handle data sent to their platform. We do not control or have visibility into how the third-party model provider processes data beyond generating and returning the result image.

## Data we do NOT collect

- No cookies or tracking scripts
- No analytics that identify individual users
- No advertising or data sharing with advertisers
- No persistent storage of uploaded images

## Your rights

Since no personal data is stored on our servers, there is nothing to request deletion of after your session ends. If you have concerns, you can simply avoid uploading photos you're not comfortable sharing with the third-party AI service.

## Changes to this policy

This is a student/portfolio project, not a commercial product. This policy may be updated as the project evolves. Any changes will be reflected in this file with an updated "Last updated" date.
